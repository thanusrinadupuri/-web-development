What is Fashion Designing?

Fashion designing is the art of creating beautiful clothing and lifestyle accessories. Designers study trends, choose fabrics, sketch shapes, and bring creative ideas to life. It combines art, culture, and business together.

Important Steps in Fashion Design

A designer follows these fundamental steps to create a dress:

  • Sketching: Drawing the initial ideas on paper using a pencil.
  • Fabric Selection: Picking materials like silk, cotton, or denim based on the design.
  • Stitching & Tailoring: Cutting the fabric shapes and sewing them together to make the final garment.
